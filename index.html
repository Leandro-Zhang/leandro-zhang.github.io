About Me

我是张乐涵，目前是一名计算机科学与技术专业的本科生。我对具身智能（Embodied Intelligence）和多模态大模型（Multimodal LLMs）充满热情，致力于探索智能体如何在与物理世界的交互中学习与进化。

研究方向

Research Interests

🤖

具身智能

Embodied Intelligence

探索智能体在物理世界中的感知、推理与行动能力。研究涵盖机器人操作策略学习、基于视觉的物体操纵、Sim-to-Real 迁移，以及利用多模态感知实现复杂环境下的自主决策。关注如何让机器人像人类一样，通过与环境的实时交互来学习和适应新任务。

Exploring how agents perceive, reason, and act in the physical world. My research covers robot manipulation policy learning, vision-based object manipulation, Sim-to-Real transfer, and autonomous decision-making in complex environments via multimodal perception. I focus on enabling robots to learn and adapt to new tasks through real-time interaction with the environment — much like humans do.

🧠

多模态大模型

Multimodal LLMs

研究视觉-语言-动作（VLA）联合建模，将大语言模型的推理能力扩展至多模态场景。方向包括：多模态指令跟随、视觉 grounding、视频理解与生成、以及将 LLM 作为具身智能任务规划的核心引擎。致力于构建能够同时理解图像、语言和物理动作的统一模型框架。

Researching vision-language-action (VLA) joint modeling, extending the reasoning capabilities of large language models to multimodal scenarios. Directions include multimodal instruction following, visual grounding, video understanding and generation, and leveraging LLMs as the core planning engine for embodied tasks. I aim to build unified model frameworks that simultaneously comprehend images, language, and physical actions.
